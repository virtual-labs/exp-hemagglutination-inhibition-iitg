### Procedure

1. **RBC Preparation:** Collect fresh chicken blood and wash it three times with PBS by centrifugation at 2000 rpm for 5 minutes.
2. **RBC Suspension:** Prepare a 1% chicken red blood cell (RBC) suspension in PBS and keep it at 4°C until use.
3. **NDV Antigen Standardization:** NDV stock is standardised separately by haemagglutination assay to obtain 4 HA units per 50 µL.
4. **Antibody Serial Dilution:** In a 96-well V-bottom microtiter plate, 50 µl of PBS is added to the wells, and dilute the antibody sample serially two-fold across the row.
5. **Antigen Addition:** Add 50 µL NDV containing 4 HA units (e.g., if the titer is 1:128, the 4HA dilution is 1:32) to each diluted antibody well and to the virus control well.
6. **Incubation:** Incubate the plate at room temperature for 30 minutes to allow antibody–virus interaction.
7. **RBC Addition:** Add 50 µL of 1% chicken RBC suspension to all wells, including RBC and virus controls.
8. **Final Incubation:** Incubate the plate at room temperature for 30–40 minutes without disturbance, and examine the wells for agglutination or button formation.
9. **HI Titre Recording:** Record the HI titre as the reciprocal of the highest antibody dilution showing complete inhibition of hemagglutination.
